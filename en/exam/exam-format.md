# ECSA Exam Format & Versions

This article explains how to find reliable information about the ECSA exam format and why certification versions matter. It is an independent, unofficial study resource: we are not affiliated with EC-Council, and EC-Council, CEH, and ECSA are trademarks of their respective owners. We do not reproduce official materials or exam dumps.

## Why versions matter

Certification programs evolve. Providers update objectives, tools, and exam blueprints as the security field changes. A version change can affect which topics are tested, how the exam is delivered, and which study materials are current. If you prepare with guides for an older version, you may study the wrong material or use outdated practice questions. Always confirm which version is active before you commit to a study plan or pay any fees.

## What to check on the official page

When you visit [eccouncil.org](https://www.eccouncil.org/), find the ECSA page and record these items. Do not rely on blogs, videos, or second-hand summaries for the answers:

| Item | What to confirm |
|------|-----------------|
| Current version | The active version name or number and its effective date |
| Exam code | The official identifier used when you register |
| Format | How the exam is structured and delivered |
| Duration | The time allowed |
| Question types | The kinds of items you will see |
| Pass mark | The published passing threshold |
| Languages | Which languages are offered |
| Delivery | Test-center or online proctoring options |
| Eligibility | Who may sit for the exam |
| Training requirement | Whether official training is required or recommended |

EC-Council changes versions and policies over time — verify the current exam version, format, eligibility, fees, retake rules, and renewal/CPD requirements on [eccouncil.org](https://www.eccouncil.org/) or with an authorized training partner.

## Useful examples, not ECSA facts

As a general illustration, many certification exams use multiple-choice questions and a fixed time limit. Some use lab simulations or scenario-based items. These are common patterns in the industry, not claims about the current ECSA exam — treat them only as examples of the kinds of details you should verify.

## Questions to ask an authorized training partner

- Which version of the exam does this course prepare me for?
- Is official training required for eligibility, or only recommended?
- Does the price include an exam voucher, and what exactly does it cover?
- What lab or cyber-range access is included?
- How do you handle rescheduling and retakes?
- Where can I see the official exam blueprint?
- Can you point me to the current policies in writing?

Ask for written answers and compare them with the official site. A trustworthy provider will welcome verification.
